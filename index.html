Gematria Calculator

Typing and Pasting

You can type Hebrew directly into the calculator. You can also paste Hebrew text into the display. The orange button adds a space.

⚙ Settings

Press the Settings button in the top-right corner to open the settings. From there you can switch between Light Mode and Dark Mode.

★ Mispar Katan

Press the star in the top-left corner to turn Mispar Katan on or off. The star turns gold when Mispar Katan is active.

= Save to History

Press the green = button to save your current calculation to your history. Your saved calculations remain available when you return to the calculator. Press Gematria Calculator to see the history.

Installing the App

If you are using Microsoft Edge, open the ⋯ menu, go to Tools then Apps, and choose Install this site as an app. Once installed, Gematria Calculator can be used offline.
